SERVICE BOX — STANDARD/DYNAMIC PRICING PROTECTION PLAN CALL SCRIPT (September 2026)
Used for every Standard or Dynamic pricing New Business, Upgrade and Downgrade appliance protection plan sale, on inbound and outbound calls. Text in CAPITALS below was shown in red in the script: it is mandatory.

== 1. INTRODUCTION ==
Inbound call: "Good morning/afternoon, you are through to Service Box, can I start by taking your first line of address and postcode please? And your full name?"

Outbound call (existing or previous customer): "Good morning/afternoon, can I speak to [Customer Name] please? My name is [agent] and I'm calling from Service Box, you have (FOR WINBACK: PREVIOUSLY HAD) your kitchen items covered with us."
- FOR WINBACK THE AGENT MUST STATE THE WORDS "PREVIOUSLY HAD".
- Data protection: "For Data Protection, please could you confirm your first line of address and postcode? And your full name?"

Outbound call (customer enquiry): "Good morning/afternoon, can I speak to [Customer Name] please? My name is [agent] and I'm calling from Service Box following your enquiry you made through [STATE WHERE THE ENQUIRY WAS FROM]."

== 2. PITCH / UNCOVER THE NEED ==
The agent establishes from the customer a need to take out a new plan, upgrade or downgrade an existing plan, or add an additional cover protection plan.

UPGRADES — IF THE AGENT IS UPGRADING AN EXISTING APPLIANCE PLAN, THE PITCH MUST STATE: "OK, so what we are able to do is to 'put cover onto' OR 'add cover onto' further household appliances. For the new items it would be the same level of cover as you've got, which is..."

Level of cover (benefits) to be stated:
- Unlimited engineer callouts
- Unlimited repairs
- Never pay for any parts or labour
- If we can't repair it, we will replace it with a brand-new/refurbished replacement of a similar technical specification.
- NOTE: FOR GADGETS ONLY THE AGENT MUST MENTION THE REPLACEMENT MAY BE NEW OR REFURBISHED.

MAIN EXCLUSIONS ("very standard, which are just"):
- COSMETIC DAMAGE
- MALICIOUS DAMAGE
- DAMAGE CAUSED BY ACCIDENT (IF DYNAMIC PRICING, ACCIDENTAL DAMAGE IS INCLUDED IN THE PROTECTION, SO IT IS NOT AN EXCLUSION)
- ANY INDIRECT COSTS
- 30 DAY WAIT PERIOD BEFORE YOU CAN CLAIM

UPGRADES — THE AGENT MUST STATE BEFORE PROCEEDING TO THE PAYMENT SECTION: "OK, so these items are getting added to your current plan and there will be a NEW DD for £xx.xx a month which will replace the previous one you had for £xx.xx per month."

== 3. PAYMENT ==
Route A — account details already held:
- "What date would you like your first payment to come out? I can delay any payment until the [payment date] of the month."
- "So just to confirm THIS IS A NEW / THESE ARE NEW PLAN(S) WITH A NEW DIRECT DEBIT. The plan starts today through until [end date]."
- "It will be 12 monthly payments of £xx.xx which is a total of £xx.xx to be collected with a first payment coming out on [DATE], and provides protection for [CLEARLY DETAILS THE ITEMS THAT WILL BE PROTECTED]."
- "WE WILL BE COLLECTING PAYMENTS FROM THE ACCOUNT HELD ON FILE ENDING IN [LAST 3 DIGITS OF THE ACCOUNT]. CAN YOU CONFIRM YOU ARE HAPPY TO USE THIS ACCOUNT?"
- "ARE YOU HAPPY FOR ME TO GO AHEAD AND SET THAT UP?"

Route B — new business where NO account details are held:
- "CAN I PLEASE TAKE YOUR SORT CODE AND ACCOUNT NUMBER SO I CAN SET THE PLAN UP?"
- "Great, and what date would you like your first payment to come out? I can delay any payment until the [payment date] of the month."
- "Great, and how does your name appear on the account?"
- "AND JUST BEFORE I PUT THAT THROUGH, CAN I CONFIRM YOU ARE THE ONLY ONE REQUIRED TO AUTHORISE THE SET UP OF DIRECT DEBITS ON THIS ACCOUNT?"
- "So just to confirm THIS IS A NEW / THESE ARE NEW PLAN(S) WITH A NEW DIRECT DEBIT. The plan starts today through until [end date]."
- "It will be 12 monthly payments of £xx.xx which is a total of £xx.xx to be collected with a first payment coming out on [DATE], and provides protection for [CLEARLY DETAILS THE ITEMS THAT WILL BE PROTECTED]."
- "ARE YOU HAPPY FOR ME TO GO AHEAD AND SET THAT UP?"

Both routes:
- "Your documents will be sent to the email address you have provided / to your home address."
- "As always, you have 14 days from the time you receive your plan documents to change your mind."

== 4. CONFIRMATION STATEMENTS (all mandatory) ==
1. "YOU ARE AUTOMATICALLY COVERED BY THE DIRECT DEBIT GUARANTEE. I CAN READ THAT TO YOU NOW OR YOU CAN READ IT IN YOUR DOCUMENTS?" If the customer says yes, the agent reads the Direct Debit Guarantee (section 6).
2. "I NEED TO MAKE YOU AWARE THAT THE PLANS ARE DISCRETIONARY AND NOT INSURANCE, FURTHER DETAILS ARE IN THE PAPERWORK SO HAVE A GOOD READ WHEN YOU GET THEM."
3. "CAN YOU CONFIRM THAT THE APPLIANCE(S) ARE IN GOOD WORKING ORDER?"
4. "I NEED YOU TO CONFIRM THESE APPLIANCES ARE NOT COVERED ANYWHERE ELSE AND OUTSIDE OF THE MANUFACTURER'S WARRANTY AND USED FOR DOMESTIC PURPOSES ONLY."

== 5. CLOSE ==
- "And lastly, just to confirm you're happy with everything I've mentioned today?"
- Marketing permission: "From time to time, we have new offers, products and specific deals we put on for existing customers. Can I just check whether you are happy for us to contact you about those by email, SMS and WhatsApp using the contact details we have on the system for you." [Customer has to say YES / confirm that they are happy]
- "That's all put into place for you now Mr/Mrs [name], if you require any more information, please don't hesitate to give us a call."
- "Thanks as always for using Service Box to protect your items, have a great day!"

== 6. DIRECT DEBIT GUARANTEE (read if the customer asks for it) ==
- This guarantee is offered by all banks and building societies that accept instructions to pay Direct Debits.
- If there are any changes to the amount, date or frequency of your Direct Debit, Service Box will notify you 10 working days in advance of your account being debited or as otherwise agreed.
- If you request Service Box to collect a payment, confirmation of the amount and date will be given to you at the time of the request.
- If an error is made in the payment of your Direct Debit, your bank or building society, you are entitled to a full and immediate refund of the amount paid from your bank or building society.
- If you receive a refund you are not entitled to, you must pay it back when Service Box asks you to.
- You can cancel a Direct Debit at any time by simply contacting your bank or building society. Written confirmation may be required. Please also notify us.
