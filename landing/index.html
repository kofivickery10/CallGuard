CallGuard AI sits alongside the dialler and CRM you already use. Nothing about how your advisers work has to change.

How does CallGuard AI score a call?

Each call is transcribed with the adviser and customer separated, and personal details in the transcript are replaced with tags. The AI gives each criterion on your scorecard its own verdict, with the transcript evidence it was decided on, or a note that none was found. Your corrections and example calls shape how future calls are scored.

Does it support Consumer Duty, ICOBS and MCOB?

CallGuard AI scores against your firm's own scorecard, so it checks what your compliance team sets: ICOBS 5 suitability for protection, MCOB 4.4A initial disclosure and MCOB 4.7A suitability for mortgages, the vulnerability characteristics described in FCA guidance FG21/1, and call-level evidence for the consumer understanding and consumer support outcomes (PRIN 2A.5 and 2A.6). Each verdict shows the transcript evidence behind it.

Can it score a sale made over several calls?

Yes, on the Pro plan. When a sale is recorded in Zoho CRM, CallGuard AI gathers that customer's calls from the sale window and scores them together as one sale, so a disclosure made on an earlier call counts when the application is submitted on a later one.

How is customer data protected?

Names, payment details and health information are replaced with tags in the transcript before it is stored or scored. Call audio is kept, encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Customer data is stored in the UK, and advisers can only see their own calls. The providers that process audio and transcripts, some of them outside the UK, are named on our sub-processor list, and our Data Processing Agreement is published.

Which diallers and systems does it work with?

CloudTalk calls are pulled in and scored automatically, and results can be written back to Zoho CRM. On the Pro plan, Twilio Media Streams calls, and Amazon Connect calls through a small Lambda bridge, can stream in live. You can also upload recordings or send calls through the REST API.
