Where Recordsure wins

Recordsure has track record we cannot claim.

Recordsure is a UK company built for UK regulated firms, so there is no US-versus-UK argument to make here and we are not going to invent one. They are longer established in this market than we are, and there is one capability they have that we genuinely lack. Four things they do well:

Face-to-face capture, which we do not have

Recordsure's Capture products, including a Capture App, create a reliable audio record of in-person meetings for evidencing. CallGuard AI covers telephony, live streams and uploaded recordings only. A native mobile capture layer for field sales is on our roadmap, scoped with a design partner, and a roadmap is not a shipped product.

A long record in UK wealth management

They position themselves as proven in wealth management and sit inside TCC Group, a firm the UK advice market already knows. Newer vendors, ourselves included, have to ask you to trust them; Recordsure can point at years of doing this work instead.

Public-sector and institutional credibility

Their customers include UK government bodies such as the Home Office and UK Visas and Immigration alongside major financial institutions, with UK-based data storage running on Microsoft Azure. That is a due-diligence pack most suppliers in this category cannot assemble.

File review built for file-review teams

ReviewAI is aimed squarely at the people who review advice files: suitability assessments, compliance evidence, and risk-ranked cases that replace random sampling with a queue of the files most likely to be wrong. If your unit of work is the file rather than the call, that is the right shape of tool.

Where it falls short

Review tells you what happened. Live tells you in time.

Recordsure's centre of gravity is post-conversation: capture it, assess it, route it to a reviewer. That is exactly right for file review and exactly wrong for stopping a breach before an unsuitable product is sold. Six differences worth weighing:

Breaches caught mid-call, not at review

On streamed calls, CallGuard AI rescores the rolling transcript every 30 seconds and fires an HMAC-signed webhook to your supervisor, CRM or agent desktop only when the AI is over 75% confident a criterion has already failed. A supervisor can step in while the customer is still on the line, which is a different outcome from finding out at review.

No published price

Recordsure does not publish pricing, so the first step in evaluating them is a conversation rather than a number. CallGuard AI publishes all three plans on the pricing page: £199, £299 and £399 per seat, per month, with volume discounts and no multi-year minimum. You can build the business case before anyone calls you.

Same-day start, no implementation project

Upload your scorecard as a CSV, connect a dialer or drop in a folder of recordings, and the first calls are scored that day. For a firm of twenty advisers, an enterprise implementation cycle costs more in delay than the software costs in licence fees.

Dialer-native ingestion

First-class adapters for CloudTalk, Twilio Media Streams and AWS Connect, plus a generic WebSocket protocol for any other dialer and a REST API for pushing recording URLs. If your advice happens down a phone line, the audio arrives without anyone exporting anything.

Every verdict carries its quote

Each scorecard criterion returns pass or fail with the exact transcript line that decided it. A reviewer agrees or overturns in seconds instead of relistening, and the evidence for the audit file is assembled as a by-product rather than as a separate task.

Scoring that learns your firm's reading

When your compliance officer overturns a verdict, that correction feeds back into later scoring for your tenant alone. Over a few hundred calls the model converges on how your firm reads "suitable" and "fair value", rather than on an industry average.

Who should pick which

Pick the right tool for your actual situation.

Pick Recordsure if

Advice happens across the table as well as down the phone and you need in-person meetings captured. Your unit of work is the advice file rather than the call, you have a file-review team and a procurement process to match, and what you want from AI is a defensible record and a risk-ranked review queue rather than intervention during the conversation.

Pick CallGuard AI if

Your regulated conversations happen on the phone, you want breaches flagged mid-call rather than at review, and you want a multi-call sale judged as one compliance unit with a transcript quote under every verdict. You are a smaller or mid-sized firm, you want the price published, and you want the first calls scored today rather than after an implementation.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can CallGuard AI record face-to-face meetings?

No. We handle telephony, live streamed calls and uploaded recordings. A native mobile capture layer for field sales is on our roadmap, scoped against a design partner rather than built speculatively, and a roadmap is not a shipped product. If capturing in-person advice meetings is a requirement today, Recordsure's Capture App does that and we do not.

Do you do file review as well as call scoring?

Not in the sense a file-review team means it. Our unit of work is the call, or the multi-call sale journey, rather than the whole advice file with its documents and adviser notes. The closest thing we offer is Reconciliation, which checks what the customer told your rep on the call against what was submitted on their behalf on the application, catching a non-disclosure gap while the paperwork can still be corrected. If your review process is document-led, ReviewAI is built for that shape of work and we are not.

How does pricing compare?

Recordsure does not publish pricing, so a quote means a conversation first. CallGuard AI publishes all three plans openly at £199, £299 and £399 per seat, per month, with volume discounts and no multi-year minimum. Full details on the pricing page.

Can you score recordings we already hold?

Yes. Drag and drop a folder, poll an SFTP location, or push a recording URL over our REST API, and we will score a back book for an audit or a remediation exercise. Historic calls are judged against the same scorecard and return the same per-criterion verdicts and quote evidence as live ones.

Can we run CallGuard AI alongside Recordsure?

Yes, and for some firms that is the sensible answer. Recordsure covers meeting capture and the file-review workflow; CallGuard AI sits over the phone estate as the live scoring and evidence layer. The two are not competing for the same job, and neither one needs to own all of your audio for the other to be useful.
