Looking for an Aveni alternative?

Aveni is a Scottish AI company built for UK regulated financial services, spanning adviser productivity through to automated case review, with its own financial-services language model behind it. CallGuard AI is deliberately narrower: compliance scoring for calls and whole sale journeys, with every price published. Both of us are UK-first, so the usual "we understand the FCA and they don't" line does not apply here. This is the honest comparison.

Where Aveni wins

Aveni is the closest thing we have to a genuine peer.

Most comparison pages in this category lean on a US-versus-UK argument. That argument is simply false about Aveni, and pretending otherwise would insult the compliance director reading this. They are UK-native, FCA-literate and in several respects further ahead than we are. Four things they do well:

Built around UK regulation, not localised into it

Consumer Duty and FG21/1 vulnerability sit in the foundations of the product rather than in a configuration layer bolted on for the UK market. If your shortlist criterion is "does this vendor speak our regulator's language", Aveni passes it as comfortably as we do.

A suite, not a single product

Aveni Assist joins client meetings, captures the fact find, drafts suitability reports and populates the CRM, and a free companion iPhone app records face-to-face meetings and queues them for upload when the adviser is back in signal. Aveni Detect handles automated case review and quality assurance coverage. Assure adds real-time risk monitoring, and Approve tests AI agents before deployment. CallGuard AI does none of the adviser-productivity half at all, and nothing at the kitchen table.

Their own domain-specific model

FinLLM is Aveni's own language model for financial services. Owning the model gives them a kind of control over behaviour that we reach a different way, through per-tenant calibration on top of Claude. Both are legitimate; theirs is a bigger engineering bet and worth respecting.

Standing in the AI governance conversation

Aveni is a participant in the FCA's Supercharged Sandbox and convened an Agent Assurance Expert Council on AI governance, alongside a deeper enterprise and wealth footprint than ours. If your board wants a supplier already visible in that conversation, that is a real advantage and we are not going to talk you out of it.

Where it falls short

The things you cannot look up before you call sales.

None of what follows says the product is weak. It describes how the two companies sell, and what each one is shaped to do. Six differences that matter most if you are a smaller or mid-sized firm rather than a national wealth manager:

No published price

Aveni quotes per customer against user count, feature scope and integrations. That is ordinary enterprise practice, but it means you cannot budget, or build an internal business case, before you have booked a sales call. CallGuard AI publishes all three plans on the pricing page: £199, £299 and £399 per seat, per month, with volume discounts and no multi-year minimum.

Same-day, not same-quarter

Upload a scorecard as a CSV, connect a dialer or drop in a folder of recordings, and your first calls are scored the same day. We will not tell you how long an Aveni rollout takes, because they do not publish it and we have not run one. We will tell you ours, and you can hold us to it.

The sale, not just the call

CallGuard AI can treat every call with one customer as a single compliance unit: the whole advice journey up to the sale, scored once. A disclosure made on call one and quietly contradicted on call three is a journey-level failure that a call-by-call view passes twice.

Dialer-native ingestion

First-class adapters for CloudTalk, Twilio Media Streams and AWS Connect, plus a generic WebSocket protocol for any other dialer and a REST API for pushing recording URLs. Getting audio in is a configuration task, not an integration project.

A quote under every verdict

Each criterion returns pass or fail with the exact transcript line that decided it, so a reviewer can agree or overturn in seconds and the audit file assembles itself. The output is written to be read by your compliance officer and, eventually, by a regulator.

Corrections that change next week's scoring

When your compliance officer overturns a verdict, that correction feeds back into subsequent scoring for your tenant alone. Over a few hundred calls the model converges on your firm's reading of "suitable" and "fair value" rather than a generic one.

Who should pick which

Pick the right tool for your actual situation.

Pick Aveni if

You want one supplier covering both the adviser's admin burden and the compliance function, so fact-find capture, suitability drafting and CRM population as well as case review. You have the procurement capacity for a bespoke quote, you value a vendor with its own domain-specific model, and visible standing with the regulator matters to your board.

Pick CallGuard AI if

Your problem is specifically the calls: scoring all of them against your scorecard with quote evidence, catching breaches while the customer is still on the line, and judging a multi-call sale as one compliance unit. You are a smaller or mid-sized firm that cannot absorb an enterprise procurement cycle, and you would rather read the price than request it.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is CallGuard AI UK-based like Aveni?

Yes. We are a UK company, UK-hosted by default, with a published DPA and sub-processor list, and our scorecards lead with ICOBS 5 suitability, FG21/1 vulnerability and the four Consumer Duty outcomes. What we are not going to do is claim to be more British than Aveni. They are a Scottish company built specifically for UK regulated financial services, and nationality is not the difference between us.

Can CallGuard AI draft suitability reports or capture a fact find?

No, and it is not on our roadmap. That is what Aveni Assist is built for. CallGuard AI scores conversations for compliance and turns them into evidence, breach alerts and coaching. If lifting the adviser's admin burden matters as much to you as the compliance function does, Aveni covers ground we simply do not.

How does pricing compare?

Aveni does not publish pricing; it is quoted per customer against user count, feature scope and integrations, and they offer a 30-day free trial of Aveni Assist. CallGuard AI publishes all three plans openly at £199, £299 and £399 per seat, per month, with volume discounts and no multi-year minimum. Full details on the pricing page.

What does journey scoring actually mean?

When a sale takes four calls over three weeks, CallGuard AI can assemble those calls and score them as one compliance unit rather than four unrelated conversations. A disclosure given on the first call and contradicted on the third is judged together, the way a file reviewer would judge it, instead of passing twice in isolation.

Can we run CallGuard AI alongside Aveni?

Yes, and the overlap is smaller than it looks. Nothing stops an adviser using a productivity assistant in the client meeting while CallGuard AI acts as the scoring and evidence layer across your telephony, with per-criterion verdicts, quote evidence and a breach register. They listen to conversations for different reasons.
