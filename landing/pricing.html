Plans FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What counts as a seat?

A seat is any user account on your CallGuard AI tenant: the advisers whose calls we score, and the compliance officers, supervisors and QA leads who review the results. Billing is per account, not per call, so a seat is charged whether or not that person handled a call in the month. Seats are quoted per plan for your team size, and volume discounts apply as headcount grows. Tell us your headcount and we will confirm your exact monthly total.

Are there volume discounts?

Yes. Per-seat pricing steps down as seat count grows, from the quoted starting rate for smaller teams. Tell us your team size and we will confirm your exact rate.

Is there a minimum contract term?

No multi-year minimum is required. Annual billing attracts a discount, but you can start monthly and move to annual once the product has proved itself on your own calls.

Is transcription and AI scoring included?

Yes. Transcription, redaction, AI scoring and coaching drafts are all part of the plan; there is no separate per-minute transcription bill.

Can I trial it before committing?

Book a demo and we will score synthetic calls against a scorecard like yours. When you want to try your own recordings, we put a DPA in place first. There is no 90-day pilot, no solution architects and no integration project.
