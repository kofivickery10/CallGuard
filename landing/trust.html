Where the data lives

UK-hosted, encrypted at rest and in transit.

Hosting

United Kingdom, London region

Compute, encrypted disk storage and the managed PostgreSQL database all run on Amazon Web Services in the London region. Call audio and transcripts do not leave the UK for storage.

Encryption

AES-256-GCM at rest

Call recordings are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Client connections and inter-service traffic use TLS 1.2 or higher.

Retention

Five years by default

Retention is set per customer. The default is five years, which suits firms keeping advice evidence for the duration of a complaint window. Set it shorter or longer to match your own policy.

Redaction

Personal data is removed before the AI sees it.

This is the part most call-analysis tools do not describe, and it is the part a data protection officer asks about first. Redaction happens at transcription — at the earliest point in the pipeline — not as a filter applied afterwards.

Stripped at source, not after storage

Names, dates of birth, email addresses, postal addresses, card and account numbers and health disclosures are replaced with typed tags — [NAME_GIVEN_1], [CARD_1] — by the transcription service itself. The unredacted text is never written to our database and never reaches a language model.

Card and account numbers are a hard floor

Card, sort-code and account-number redaction cannot be disabled for any customer, under any agreement. Storing full payment numbers in a general-purpose application database would pull the whole product into PCI DSS scope, which is not a trade we will make for anyone.

Wider exemptions are DPIA-gated

A customer reconciling what was said on a call against what was submitted can need some identifiers to survive. That is possible, but it is not a self-serve toggle: it is set by us, only against a recorded DPIA reference and approver, and every change is written to the audit log. It is currently enabled for no customer.

Scoring runs on redacted text

Every AI pass — transcript cleanup, scoring, breach detection, coaching — runs against the redacted transcript. The evidence quote attached to a criterion is a quote from that redacted text, so the audit trail carries no more personal data than it needs to.

What we do not have yet

The honest half of a trust page.

CallGuard AI does not hold ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials Plus or a SOC 2 report. Some vendors in this category do. We would rather say so here than have you discover it in a questionnaire.

What we would put against that: the controls above are specific, checkable and written down, which a certificate does not by itself tell you. The registration number is verifiable on the ICO register. The sub-processor list names every party and where each one sits. The redaction behaviour is described precisely enough that you can test it against a call of your own during a trial, which is the only assurance that actually proves anything.

If a certification is a hard requirement for your procurement process, tell us at security@callguardai.co.uk and we will give you a straight answer about timing rather than a maybe.
