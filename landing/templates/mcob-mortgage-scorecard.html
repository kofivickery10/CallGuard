FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is this scorecard FCA-approved or endorsed?

No. It is a starting template, not an FCA-endorsed standard, and using it does not guarantee compliance. It exists so a firm building its first AI-scored QA programme is editing a scorecard on day one rather than starting from a blank page. Reword criteria, change weights and severities, delete what does not apply and add what your permissions or lender panel demand.

What does the weight and severity on each item mean?

Weight (1, 1.5 or 2) sets how much a criterion counts toward the overall percentage score. Severity (low, medium, high or critical) is separate: a critical item failing can flag the whole call for review regardless of the overall score, so a missed early repayment charge explanation does not carry the same consequence as a missed pleasantry.

What does consent-gated mean?

A clear affirmative from the customer is required, and it cannot be inferred from silence or from the adviser simply saying the words. No unambiguous yes in the recording, no pass. Where the AI is not confident either way, the item routes to a manual review queue for a human to decide rather than being auto-scored, because a false pass on a consent item is the worst outcome available.
