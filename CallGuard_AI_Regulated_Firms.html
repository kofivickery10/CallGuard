How it works
How it works

Calls in. Compliance evidence out.

CallGuard sits alongside the telephony you already use. There is no rip-and-replace, no new dialler, and no integration project — whether your team uses a cloud dialler, a compliance recorder, Microsoft Teams, or simply uploads recorded advice files.

1

Bring in your calls, however they arrive

Drag-and-drop upload, automated collection from your recording server, a webhook from your dialler, or our REST API. CloudTalk and Zoho CRM connect natively; Twilio and AWS Connect stream live. Audio is encrypted the moment it lands.

2

Score against your scorecard, not a generic one

Upload your existing QA scorecard — the one your compliance team already uses. Add your suitability rules, product guides and call standards to the knowledge base. Every call is transcribed with the adviser and customer separated, then judged criterion by criterion against your firm's standard.

3

Act on it, and keep the proof

Pass or fail per criterion with a supporting quote, a weighted overall score, a breach register, and a coaching brief for the adviser. Critical breaches alert by email, Slack or webhook. Results write back to your CRM. Everything is retained as the audit record.

It learns your firm's interpretation, not a textbook's

Where the AI is uncertain, or where a consent step is borderline, the call is routed to a review queue rather than being scored automatically — no false pass, no false breach. When your compliance officer corrects a verdict, that correction becomes a worked example that shapes future scoring. Mark your best calls as exemplars and they set the reference standard. After a few dozen corrections, CallGuard scores the way your senior reviewer scores. Your interpretation of "suitable", "fair value" and "good outcome" is the one that counts.

The platform

Five things CallGuard does that a QA spreadsheet cannot.

Every call scored, with the proof attached

Not a sample — every conversation. Each criterion on your scorecard returns a pass or fail, a confidence level, and the exact sentence from the transcript that justifies it. You are never asked to take the AI's word for it; you can read the line it relied on.

We score the sale, not just the call

A single recommendation usually spans three or four conversations. CallGuard joins them into one customer journey and assesses compliance across the whole thing — so a disclosure made on the second call still counts, and one missed everywhere is caught. That is how the regulator looks at an outcome, so it is how we score it.

Breaches caught mid-call, not next week

For teams that stream live, CallGuard listens as the conversation happens and flags a critical compliance failure before the adviser hangs up — only when it is confident, so supervisors are not swamped with noise. A breach prevented costs nothing to remediate.

Customer personal data never reaches the AI

Names, addresses, card numbers and health details are stripped out at the moment of transcription and replaced with typed tags. They are never stored in our system and never sent to a language model. It is the first question your DPO will ask, and it is designed into the pipeline rather than bolted on afterwards.

The audit pack, on demand

When the FCA, your auditor, your principal or the Ombudsman asks you to show your work, export it: every call, every verdict, every supporting quote, every correction your compliance team made, and who made it. What used to be a file-review project becomes a download.

Data protection
Security & data protection

Built for a firm that has to answer for its processors.

You are accountable for your data processors as well as your advisers. These are the answers your DPO, your IT reviewer and your principal will ask for — set out plainly so you can pass this page straight to them.

Personal data is removed before storage
Names, addresses, postcodes, phone numbers, card and bank details and health information are detected and replaced with typed tags at the point of transcription. The raw values are never written to our database and never form part of any AI prompt.
Audio encrypted at rest
AES-256-GCM encryption for every stored recording, with keys held separately from the data.
Encrypted in transit
TLS 1.2 or above for all connections, including dialler webhooks, live audio streams and CRM write-back.
UK data residency
Data is hosted in the UK, and speech processing is pinned to the EU region with model-improvement use explicitly opted out.
Role-based access, with an audit log
Administrator, supervisor, viewer and adviser roles; advisers see only their own calls. Two-factor authentication is available, and privileged actions are written to an immutable audit log.
Your retention policy, enforced automatically
Set the retention period your firm requires. Recordings and transcripts are purged on schedule without anyone having to remember, and deletion is recorded.
UK GDPR Article 28 DPA
A data processing agreement is available on request, and our sub-processor list is published publicly — so you know exactly who touches your data before you sign anything.
Your data is not used to train models
Improvement using anonymised customer-derived data is opt-in, off by default, and recorded with a timestamp if you ever choose to enable it.
