How this page is built

The main decision

Two states, not one compromise

The last prototype kept the sale as a dense two-pane tool and I said airy and dense pull against each other. They do — but they are two different jobs, so they get two states. The sale page is where you decide: score, findings, evidence quote, act. Focus mode is where you read the transcript alongside the checkpoints. “Read alongside the transcript” hands off between them, and any “See in transcript” link goes straight there with that checkpoint selected.

Most reviews never need the transcript

That's what makes the split work. The quote, the timestamp, and a play button from that second are usually enough to judge a finding. So the default state gives you those inline and keeps the full transcript one click away, instead of spending half the screen on it every time.

Four stat cards, each a jump

Score with its meter and the delta from the previous run; checkpoints as a pass/fail strip so the shape is visible before the number; breaches; calls. Clicking one scrolls to the card that explains it — the same rule as the home page, where a tile you can't act on is a poster.

Findings are grouped by section, failures first

“Disclosure — 1 of 3 failed” tells you where the problem lives before you read a word of it. Failures and consent gates open expanded; everything that passed folds into one row, because 22 passes are a reassurance, not reading.

Each finding carries its own evidence and its own actions

Quote, who said it and when, play from that second, why the AI judged it that way, and the action — overturn, note, or confirm/reject for a consent gate. No modal: the decision happens where the evidence is.

The caveats sit above the findings

An unconfirmed branch changes which checkpoints applied at all, and unreliable speaker labels are why two checkpoints are unscored. Both change how the score should be read, so they come before it rather than in a footnote.

Capture, reconciliation, feedback and history are cards, not panels

Each shows a one-line summary and a count, and opens in place with a breadcrumb back. The same card-hub pattern as the Set up page, so the gesture is learned once. An unused module costs one card rather than a screenful of empty panel.

The calls card says why it has no scores

Under sale scoring the member calls rest unscored on purpose. Left blank that reads as a bug, so the card says it in a line.

Prototype. Same tokens as the airy prototype — all text clears 4.5:1 on every surface in both themes. Names and figures invented.
