What changed, and why

Try this first

The Proposed / Today switch

Top right. It swaps only CSS custom properties — colour, type scale, radii, shadows, and whether micro-labels are uppercase. No markup changes, no component knows which set is live. That is the audit's central claim made testable: the repaint really is one file.

Green means passed, and nothing else

The brand moves to a deep ink so a green element can only ever mean “this complied”. Today brand green and pass green contrast against each other at 1.86:1 — the interface literally cannot tell you which it means.

Every colour is measured

All text clears 4.5:1 on every surface it sits on, in both themes, including text on its own badge wash. Today's text-muted is 2.86:1 — it fails even the large-text floor, and it is the colour of every table header and timestamp.

Verdicts are never colour alone

Pass, fail and review each carry an icon and a word. Red, amber and green cannot be made separable for a deuteranopic reader — the best re-stepping still leaves them close — so form does the work colour can't.

Page height no longer depends on the data

Checkpoints and transcript are two bound panes that scroll inside the viewport. A forty-minute call and a four-minute call produce the same page. Click any checkpoint: the transcript jumps to its quote and the dock's playhead moves with it.

The two things used together are together

Capture, reconciliation, feedback and history are separate jobs, so they are tabs with counts. Evidence work is one job, so it is one split view. An unused module now costs a tab, not a screenful of empty panel.

Passes collapse; failures don't

Four items need a human. Twenty-five don't, and fold into one row. Today every checkpoint renders its full evidence and reasoning whether or not anyone needs to read it.

Reading measure, and timestamps

Transcript turns cap at 68 characters instead of running the full 1,400px, each turn carries a timestamp, cited lines are marked, and redaction tags render as chips rather than raw bracket text.

The queue is a mode, not a page

“3 of 27” with arrows sits in the context header, so resolving an item advances you rather than returning you to a list. Keyboard hints live on the dock.

Uncertainty gets more weight, not less

The caveat block keeps its place above the result, and low-confidence items say what the AI suggested, why it isn't trusted, and what to do about it.
