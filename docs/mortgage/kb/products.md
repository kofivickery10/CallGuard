# Mortgage products and terminology

Knowledge Base context so the transcript cleanup pass and the scorer read
mortgage vocabulary correctly, and so a recommendation can be judged against what
the product actually is. Sector baseline — replace or extend with the firm's own
panel and product range.

## Case types

- **Purchase** — a new property. First-time buyer, home mover, or new build.
- **Remortgage** — moving an existing mortgage to a different lender, usually at
  the end of a deal period, sometimes to release equity.
- **Product transfer** — staying with the current lender and switching to a new
  rate. Usually cheaper and quicker than remortgaging, with no new affordability
  assessment in many cases, which is exactly why it has to be compared against
  remortgaging rather than ignored.
- **Further advance** — additional borrowing from the existing lender.
- **Buy-to-let** — consumer BTL is regulated; most business BTL is not.

## Repayment method

- **Capital repayment** — payments cover interest and capital; the balance reaches
  zero at the end of the term.
- **Interest-only** — payments cover interest only; the full balance is
  outstanding at the end of the term and needs a repayment strategy (sale of
  another asset, investments, pension lump sum). Recommending interest-only
  without a credible, stated strategy is a critical failure.
- **Part and part** — a mix of the two.

## Rate types

- **Fixed rate** — the rate is fixed for an initial period (commonly 2, 3, 5 or 10
  years). Payment certainty; an early repayment charge normally applies during the
  fixed period.
- **Tracker** — tracks the Bank of England base rate at a set margin. The payment
  moves with the base rate.
- **Discounted variable** — a discount off the lender's own variable rate, which
  the lender can change at its discretion.
- **Standard variable rate (SVR)** — the lender's default rate, which the mortgage
  reverts to when the initial period ends. Almost always more expensive, which is
  why the reversion must be explained.
- **Offset** — savings held with the lender reduce the interest charged.

## Terms the scorer and cleanup pass should recognise

Loan to value (LTV), decision in principle (DIP), agreement in principle (AIP),
ESIS (European Standardised Information Sheet), KFI, illustration, early
repayment charge (ERC), arrangement fee, product fee, booking fee, valuation fee,
procuration fee, retention product, porting, portability, overpayment allowance,
stamp duty land tax, SDLT, affordability assessment, stress test, gifted deposit,
Help to Buy, shared ownership, leasehold, freehold, conveyancing, searches,
exchange, completion, joint application, sole applicant, guarantor,
self-employed, SA302, day-one remortgage, capital raising, debt consolidation,
adverse credit, CCJ, default, payday loan, credit search, hard search, soft
search.

## Costs that must be named, not just implied

Monthly payment, total amount payable, total interest over the term, ERC amount
and expiry, product fee (and whether added to the loan), valuation fee, legal and
conveyancing costs, broker fee.

## Common mishearings on 8kHz telephony

- "ESIS" → heard as "ISIS", "E-S-I-S", "assist"
- "ERC" → "E R C", "easy"
- "LTV" → "L T V", "elty"
- "SVR" → "S V R", "server"
- "procuration fee" → "procreation fee", "proc fee"
- "porting" → "pawning", "porting it"
- "DIP" / "AIP" → "dip", "a i p"
- "SDLT" → "S D L T"
