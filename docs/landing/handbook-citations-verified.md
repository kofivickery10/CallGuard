## Deliberately NOT verified

Do not treat any of these as checked, and do not let a later pass assume they
were, because a second failed attempt looks identical to a success in a diff:

- **Any ico.org.uk URL.** The site returned 403 to every request, so no ICO
  page has been opened. Every claim about ICO enforcement, penalty sizes or
  the language in enforcement notices is unsourced.
- **The body text of FG21/1.** The PDF has no extractable text layer. Only the
  landing page has been read, so quotations from inside the guidance are
  unconfirmed. Note also that on 22 July 2026 the FCA added a note saying
  FG21/1 contains outdated pre-Consumer Duty references.
- **ICOBS 7 and COBS 15 cancellation periods.** Cooling-off is
  product-dependent; "14 days" is not a general minimum.
- **ICOBS 5.1.1G as a disclosure provision** (checked 2026-09-16). It is about
  eligibility to claim benefits (qualifying requirements, age limits), not
  disclosure. Do not cite it for non-disclosure.
- **ICOBS 8.1.2, 8.1.2A and 8.1.2B.** Reads conflicted on whether each is a
  rule or guidance (2026-09-16). Do not cite until settled.
- **Whether a newer ABI misrepresentation Code exists.** The July 2023 edition
  was due for review by July 2026 and no later edition could be confirmed
  (2026-09-16). Always call it the July 2023 edition, and voluntary.
- **Any adviser duty to record calls, check the application against the call,
  or monitor disclosure answers.** Nothing verified imposes one (2026-09-16).

## Rules that keep being got wrong

Recorded here because each one was found on a live page:

1. **PECR does not require consent for live marketing calls.** Reg 21 prohibits
   a call only where the subscriber has told the caller to stop, or the number
   is TPS-listed for 28 days or more. Consent is the gate for reg 19 (automated
   calls), reg 21A (claims management) and reg 21B (pensions).
2. **Soft opt-in is reg 22, which is about electronic mail.** It has nothing to
   say about live calls.
3. **Reg 24 requires the caller's name.** An address or a freephone number is
   required only if the recipient asks. It says nothing about the purpose of
   the call, and imposes no timing test.
4. **Call-recording disclosure is not PECR.** It sits in UK GDPR Art 13/14 and,
   for some firms, the FCA taping rules.
5. **The in-call right to object to direct marketing is UK GDPR Art 21(2)-(3)**,
   not PECR reg 21, which bites on later calls.
6. **Only PRIN 2A.5 and 2A.6 are visible on a call.** PRIN 2A.3 binds
   manufacturers and distributors through product governance, and PRIN 2A.4
   binds manufacturers to run a value assessment — and 2A.4 is disapplied
   altogether for non-investment insurance, which is most protection business.
7. **PRIN 2A.5.9R says "ask" whether the customer understands**, qualified by
   "where appropriate". Asking the customer to paraphrase the recommendation
   back is a good evidence standard to recommend, but it is ours, not the
   FCA's.
8. **Consumer Duty came into force 31 July 2023 for open products and
   31 July 2024 for closed ones.**
9. **ICOBS covers general insurance and pure protection.** An investment advice
   example needs COBS.
10. **Energy supply is regulated by Ofgem, not the FCA.**
11. **The CIDRA duty is the consumer's, owed to the insurer** (s2). An adviser
    does not owe it. The "clear and specific question" wording is s3(2)(c) and
    s5(5)(b), not s3(4).
