Notes on the 2026-09-16 rows:

- **ICOBS 5.2 applies to every sale, advised or not** (5.2.1R, 5.2.2DR "must always",
  5.2.2CG "whether or not advice is given"). **ICOBS 5.3 applies to advised sales**, with
  two different triggers: 5.3.1R by "advice", 5.3.4R only by a "personal recommendation".
  Keep them distinct.
- **No provision in ICOBS 5 addresses replacement business.** 5.1, 5.2 and 5.3 were
  searched for "replace", "replacement", "switch" and "cancel". The only related wording is
  "including details of existing insurance cover" in ICOBS 5.3.2G(1)(a): guidance, advised
  pure protection only.
- **CP26/22's page names no ICOBS provision.** Its advised-sales proposal is "removing
  references to 'advice' that do not involve a personal recommendation". Say only that, and
  attribute no specific ICOBS change to it.

## Rules that keep being got wrong

Recorded here because each one was found on a live page:

1. **PECR does not require consent for live marketing calls.** Reg 21 prohibits
   a call only where the subscriber has told the caller to stop, or the number
   is TPS-listed for 28 days or more. Consent is the gate for reg 19 (automated
   calls), reg 21A (claims management) and reg 21B (pensions).
2. **Soft opt-in is reg 22, which is about electronic mail.** It has nothing to
   say about live calls.
3. **Reg 24 requires the caller's name.** An address or a freephone number is
   required only if the recipient asks. It says nothing about the purpose of
   the call, and imposes no timing test.
4. **Call-recording disclosure is not PECR.** It sits in UK GDPR Art 13/14 and,
   for some firms, the FCA taping rules.
5. **The in-call right to object to direct marketing is UK GDPR Art 21(2)-(3)**,
   not PECR reg 21, which bites on later calls.
6. **Only PRIN 2A.5 and 2A.6 are visible on a call.** PRIN 2A.3 binds
   manufacturers and distributors through product governance, and PRIN 2A.4
   binds manufacturers to run a value assessment — and 2A.4 is disapplied
   altogether for non-investment insurance, which is most protection business.
7. **PRIN 2A.5.9R says "ask" whether the customer understands**, qualified by
   "where appropriate". Asking the customer to paraphrase the recommendation
   back is a good evidence standard to recommend, but it is ours, not the
   FCA's.
8. **Consumer Duty came into force 31 July 2023 for open products and
   31 July 2024 for closed ones.**
9. **ICOBS covers general insurance and pure protection.** An investment advice
   example needs COBS.
10. **Energy supply is regulated by Ofgem, not the FCA.**
11. **Not every protection sale is under ICOBS.** A firm that has elected to apply COBS
    to its pure protection business is outside ICOBS (except ICOBS 4.6) for that business
    (ICOBS 1 Annex 1 Part 2, 3.1R). Write "firms selling protection under ICOBS", never
    "every protection sale is subject to ICOBS 5.2", and do not name the COBS rules that
    apply instead.
12. **Demands and needs is not suitability.** ICOBS 5.2 applies to every sale, advised or
    not; ICOBS 5.3 applies only where advice is given. ICOBS 5.2.2AG is guidance ("may"),
    and the pure protection suitability provision is ICOBS 5.3.2G(1)(a), not "5.3.2G(a)".
